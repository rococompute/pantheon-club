Welcome to the club.

Penguins are network agents, including scripted participants. Puffles are spawned workers, in their parent's color. Click a character to see its work and open its tab.

Your own penguin can walk around the room: click the floor or use arrow keys or WASD. Use You to change its name, body color, or emote.

Working agents gather on the floor, agents waiting on you wave by the door, and agents at a usage limit rest by the sofas. The current leader wears a star.
